Clubs:

MATHS

Also known as the Mathematics Association of Talented High schoolers of RSHS-IX, this club specializes in math-based activities and events. This club encourages students to show of their natural talent and passion for math.

BYR

Also known as Baile y' Ritmo (Dance and Rhythm), this club specializes in dancing for major events and competitions, this club will represent RSHS-IX in dancing competitions. This club encourages students to show off their talent for dancing.

Eunoia Agon

Is a debate club that was recently established to encourage studebts to show their natural talent for debating.

Peace Club

Is a club that encourages peace within and outside the school campus.

SWAT

Also known as the Society of Witts and Talents, this is an english-based club that encourages learners to show their interests or fond for english literature. This club specializes in all english-based events and activities.

KPAP

Also known as Kabataan Para sa Adhikain Pangwikang filipino, this is a filipino-based club that encourages students to show their interests or fond for filipino literature. This club specializes in all filipino-based events and activities.

ROAR

Also known as Resillient Outstanding Athletes of RSHS-IX, this club specializes in all sports events and activities and will be representing RSHS-IX for regional and divisional competitions. This club also encourages students to show their talent and fond for sports.

SINAG Prodigies

Also known as The Society of Imaginative, Nascent, and Gifted Prodigies, this club specializes in all art-based events and activities and encourages students to shown the natural talent and passion for art

GAD

Also known as Gender and Development Club, this club encourages gender equality within and outside the school campus.

SSS

Also known as the Social Studies Society, this club specializes in all society and historical related activities and events .
